How it works

From syllabus chaos to crystal clarity

Three simple ideas that compound into a powerful system you'll actually stick with.

1
Paste your syllabus
Drop in your raw syllabus text. StudyOS auto-detects modules, units, and topics — no formatting needed. Review the preview, then save with one click.
2
Track every session
Start the timer when you open a textbook. Stop it when you close it. Your sessions are automatically tagged to the right subject and module.
3
Watch your progress
Mark topics done as you go. Watch the progress bars fill. See your streak grow. Understand exactly how much you've covered and what's left.
4
Stay in focus
Enter Focus Mode for a distraction-free timer view. Set a daily goal. Build the study habit that actually gets you through exam season.
